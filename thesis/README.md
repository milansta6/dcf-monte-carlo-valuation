# The thesis

**Company Valuation Using the Discounted Cash Flow Method and Monte Carlo Simulation**
Master's thesis, Master in Computer Science – Computational Finance,
School of Computing, Union University, Belgrade, 2026.
Author: Milan Stamenović · Mentor: Prof. dr Branko Urošević

📄 [Master_Thesis_Milan_Stamenovic_public.pdf](Master_Thesis_Milan_Stamenovic_public.pdf)

## Public version

This is the public version of the thesis. The second case study (Company B,
a furniture manufacturer) concerns a confidential client. In this version, figures
that could identify the company (exact balance-sheet amounts, details of the
management forecast and a few descriptive details) have been rounded or removed.
The methodology, all percentages, the results in EUR millions and the
conclusions are unchanged.
